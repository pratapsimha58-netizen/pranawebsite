## The short answer

New coaches book a first client when the offer is clear in plain language, the invite is practised without pushiness, and the first session has a simple structure. Training alone rarely creates that — deliberate practise does.

## Why do trained coaches still freeze at outreach?

Fear of rejection, unclear positioning, and hustle advice that feels unlike them. The fix is rehearsed, human language — not louder funnels.

A soft invite names who you help, the outcome, and a low-pressure next step (a short conversation). It does not beg or overpromise.

## What belongs in the first paid session?

A clear opening, listening for the client’s goal, one useful insight or practice, and a kind close. Structure reduces nerves for both of you.

## A small exercise

Write your offer in two sentences a friend would understand. Practise saying it aloud twice. Then list three people you could invite to a discovery chat this month.
