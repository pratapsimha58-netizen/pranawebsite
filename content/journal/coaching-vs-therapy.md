## The short answer

Therapy addresses clinical mental-health needs with licensed clinicians. Coaching partners with you on goals, habits, confidence, and life design when you are generally able to function and want practical support. If you are unsure, start with a clinician — coaches should refer, not replace care.

## When is coaching a good fit?

Coaching fits lifestyle change, fitness habits, resilience in busy seasons, confidence after a hard phase, and early coaching-business clarity. Sessions focus on practice and accountability, not diagnosis.

Choose therapy (or both) when trauma processing, clinical anxiety or depression, addiction treatment, or acute crisis is primary. Prana Way will say so kindly if coaching is not the right container.

## How do coaching and therapy work together?

Some people work with both: therapy for clinical depth, coaching for weekly habits and goals. They are complementary when roles stay clear.

## A practical takeaway

Ask yourself: do I need healing for symptoms that impair daily life, or a partner for goals I already understand? If both, begin with clinical care and add coaching when ready.
