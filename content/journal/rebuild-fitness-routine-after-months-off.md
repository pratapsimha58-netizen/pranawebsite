## The short answer

Rebuild fitness by restoring sleep and a tiny movement habit before chasing old PRs. After months off, the body and calendar have changed — a coach helps you design a rhythm you can keep on ordinary weeks.

## Why does going hard again usually fail?

Returning at your previous intensity often leads to soreness, guilt, and another stop. The gap is not motivation alone; it is capacity and a plan that ignores real life.

## What should the first two weeks look like?

Protect sleep anchors, add walking or light strength you can finish even on busy days, and track energy — not perfection. {{TODO: owner — optional sample week if you want one public}}

## How is coaching different from an app?

An app assigns workouts. Coaching notices what stops you, adjusts the week, and keeps accountability kind when you miss a day.

## A practical takeaway

This week: three 20-minute walks and one earlier bedtime. That is enough to restart trust with yourself.
