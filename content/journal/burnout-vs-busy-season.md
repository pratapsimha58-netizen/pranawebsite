## The short answer

A busy season has a visible end and you still recover on rest days. Burnout is when rest stops restoring you, patience thins, and the cost shows up in sleep, habits, and relationships. If you are unsure, treat recovery as non-negotiable and seek clinical help when symptoms are severe.

## What does a healthy busy season feel like?

Demanding, but temporary. You can still sleep somewhat, connect with people, and imagine the finish line.

Persistent exhaustion after rest, cynicism, reduced efficacy, and coping habits that leave you worse. These are signals to slow down and get support — coaching and/or clinical care.

## What can you change this week without quitting your job?

Protect one recovery block, reduce one draining loop, and ask for one boundary at work or home. Small, kept promises rebuild capacity.

## A practical takeaway

Rate your last three rest days from 1–5 on how restored you felt. If the average is low, you may be past “busy” and into depletion.

## When coaching helps

[Resilience coaching](/resilience-coaching) helps you rebuild steadiness for stress and setbacks when you are still able to work and want a practical partner — not a substitute for therapy when clinical care is needed.
