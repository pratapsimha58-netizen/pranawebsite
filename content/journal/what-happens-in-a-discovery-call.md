## The short answer

A discovery call is a short, low-pressure conversation to see whether coaching fits. At Prana Way it is about listening — what feels heavy, what you miss about yourself, and what “better” would feel like — not a sales pitch.

Calls are typically about thirty minutes on Google Meet. You can join from Bengaluru or anywhere online.

## What will I be asked?

Expect gentle questions about your primary goal, what you have already tried, and what a good week would feel like. You will not be asked to commit on the spot.

## What happens after the call?

If there is a fit and a seat, you choose a package and a start date. If not, you leave with clarity — and a referral if another form of support is better.

## A small exercise before you book

Write three sentences: where you are, what you miss, and one thing you will not do to “fix” yourself. Bring that to the call.
