# Market and customer

## Market sizing (bottom-up, assumptions tagged)

The point of sizing here is not to claim a large TAM but to check that a solo business reaching INR 2-3 lakh a month does not need a meaningful market share.

| Step | Figure | Basis | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White-collar professionals in India with 2-8 years of experience in IT, product, BFSI, consulting and analytics | ~12 million | Roughly 5.4 million in IT services alone (NASSCOM headcount), plus product, BFSI and consulting; filtered to the 2-8 year band | M-1 |
| Share actively switching jobs in a given year | 20% | IT services attrition has ranged from 12% to 25% a year over the past five years; product companies higher in good years | M-2 |
| Active switchers per year | ~2.4 million | M-1 x M-2 | |
| Share willing to pay for any career help | 5% | Naukri FastForward, Preplaced, Topmate and freelancer volumes suggest single-digit paid penetration | M-3 |
| Paying customers per year (serviceable market) | ~120,000 | | |
| Average spend | INR 3,500 | Between resume-only and bundle pricing | M-4 |
| Serviceable market value | ~INR 42 crore a year | | |

At INR 2.5 lakh a month (INR 30 lakh a year) the business needs roughly 850 orders a year, or 0.7% of the serviceable market. Even if M-2 and M-3 are both half as large, the target is under 3% share. The market is not the constraint; distribution is.

## Why demand exists now

1. **ATS adoption.** Most mid and large Indian employers screen with applicant tracking systems (Naukri RMS, Workday, Greenhouse, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit). Candidates have learned that formatting kills applications before a human sees them, which is the same anxiety that built TopResume.
2. **Switching is the main way to get a raise.** Typical in-role appraisals run 6-12%; switches run 30-100% for the 2-8 year band. That makes a few thousand rupees on preparation an obvious investment.
3. **Layoff cycles.** Every round of startup and IT layoffs since 2022 has produced a spike in "resume review" requests on LinkedIn and Reddit. Demand is bursty, which favours a lean operator who can absorb spikes with contracted writers.
4. **Cheap supply is untrustworthy.** Fiverr and Instagram resume writers deliver templated output with no accountability. AI tools produce generic text that recruiters now recognise. Neither includes a human who asks the right questions.
5. **Payment habits.** UPI and Razorpay links make a INR 2,499 purchase frictionless; there is no card-adoption barrier that existed five years ago.

Launch focus: the first two segments, because they cluster in a few online communities and refer each other.

## Where buyers spend time

- **LinkedIn**: the only platform where career content is expected. Founder posts on resume mistakes, before/after bullets and interview stories build authority.
- **Telegram job channels**: hundreds of channels with 10,000-200,000 members share openings; members are actively applying and receptive to resume help.
- **Reddit**: r/developersIndia, r/IndianWorkplace, r/india_jobs. Resume-review threads are frequent; honest, useful comments earn DMs.
- **WhatsApp alumni and college groups**: referral engine once a few members have results.
- **YouTube comments and Discord** on channels covering placements and switching.

## Buying journey

1. Trigger event (appraisal, layoff, friend's offer).
2. Applies to 20-50 roles, gets few or no responses.
3. Searches "ATS resume", "resume review India", asks in a community.
4. Sees founder content or a free ATS score offer, submits resume.
5. Receives score plus three specific fixes on WhatsApp within 24 hours; is offered a package.
6. Buys the package (typically Resume + LinkedIn) after a short conversation.
7. Intake call, draft, revisions, review call, delivery in 3-4 days.
8. Applies again; if interviews come, buys mock interviews; refers colleagues.

Sales cycle target: under 7 days from first contact to purchase.

| Naukri FastForward | 1,800-6,000 | Distribution, brand | Templated output, no live call, slow turnaround, no interview prep |

Positioning statement: *ShiftReady is the job-switch service for Indian professionals with 2-8 years of experience who want interviews, not a template. Every package includes a human who asks the right questions, a Naukri- and ATS-ready format, and a call-back guarantee.*
